HOW TO PLAY

Keep the ball in play, break every brick, and clear all 50 stages.

CONTROLS

  • ← / → or A / D — move the paddle. Touch players can use the on-screen arrows.
  • P or ESC — pause or resume.
  • J — unlock the next juice at a milestone, or cycle through juices you have unlocked.
  • S — cycle ball speed while using the Admin skin.

Catch the ball with your paddle to send it back into the bricks. Falling balls cost a life. Catch falling power-ups for a wider paddle, a slower ball, or an extra life.

JUICE MODES

Juice adds visual feedback on brick breaks and paddle hits; sound effects play separately. It does not change the rules. Break 50 bricks per unlock, then press J to unlock and select the next juice.
